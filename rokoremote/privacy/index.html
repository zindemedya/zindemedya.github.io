Privacy Policy

RokoRemote is an independent app, not affiliated with, approved, developed or sponsored by Roku, Inc. Roku is a trademark of its owner.

1

RokoRemote controls compatible Roku devices on your local network. No account is needed. Zinde Medya does not collect viewing history, remote button history, contacts, messages or GPS location.

2

The app may keep a device's local IP address, name, favorites and preferences on your phone. Commands go directly to the chosen device on the local network, not to a Zinde Medya server.

3

The free version may show Google AdMob ads when activated. Google's advertising services may process device or advertising identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions and diagnostics under their policies. Where required, consent options are offered through Google UMP.

4

Optional one-time Remove Ads purchases are processed by Google Play. Zinde Medya does not receive your payment card details. The app checks your purchase entitlement to restore Premium.

5

You can generally remove local preferences by clearing app data or uninstalling. Google and GitHub Pages (hosting these pages) handle their own technical data under their policies. For privacy requests use Zinde Medya's developer contact on Google Play.
