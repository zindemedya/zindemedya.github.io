Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how WeboRemote – Remote for LG TV, provided by Zinde Medya, handles information.

1. How the app works

WeboRemote controls compatible LG webOS TVs over the same local network. Your phone and TV must normally be connected to the same local network.

2. Information handled by WeboRemote

No WeboRemote account is required. Zinde Medya does not operate an app server that receives your remote-control history, watched content, channel history, contacts, or messages. Technical information needed for operation, such as a TV name, local IP address, and webOS pairing/client key, may be stored locally on your device.

3. Local network access

The app uses local-network connectivity to discover, pair with, and control compatible TVs. Local TV connection information is used to provide the remote-control function and is not uploaded by WeboRemote to Zinde Medya for advertising.

4. Advertising

The free version may display Google AdMob banner advertising. Google Mobile Ads services may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information under Google's policies. Where required, advertising consent and privacy choices are managed through Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).

5. Premium purchase

The one-time “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Google Play or the relevant app store. Zinde Medya does not receive your card number or complete payment credentials. The app may store an entitlement status locally and use the store to verify or restore purchases.

6. Website hosting

Privacy, terms, and support pages are hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard technical access information according to its own policies when these pages are visited.

7. Retention and deletion

Locally stored pairing and preference data can generally be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Information processed independently by Google or an app store is subject to those providers' retention and deletion rules.

8. Children

WeboRemote is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Platform rules apply to advertising and store services.

9. Your choices

Where required, advertising privacy choices can be reopened from the app's Settings → Ad Privacy Options. Rights available under applicable privacy law may also be exercised with the relevant service provider.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app, SDKs, or legal requirements change. For support or privacy requests, use the Zinde Medya developer contact shown in the relevant app-store listing.
